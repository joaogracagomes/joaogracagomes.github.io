How the numbers are made
  • Engine: Step 5 of Nuclear_Fusion_TEA_baseline.ipynb, run in the browser. Pre-tax NPV in real 2024 dollars, triangular inputs, 20,000 futures: half set the developer's stop rules, half report results.
  • Plant: 350 MWe, $16,342–44,360/kW, about 30% availability, O&M $35–182/MWh, 30-year life (Araiinejad & Shirvan 2025, via the brief).
  • Stage 1: the team's demo-plant model (4,000 builds × 139 scenarios), fixed at FERMI's settings. The bar at the top changes stage 2 only.
  • Check: with the full credit and a $250 buyers' price, stage 2 costs $4.47B and brings $0.34 per taxpayer dollar, as in the notebook.
